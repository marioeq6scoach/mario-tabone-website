Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how Mario Tabone Coaching, operated by Mario Tabone, handles personal information through mariotabone.com.au, including when you request the free e-book, contact me, or choose to book a session.

Information collected

  • Free guide signup: your email address, whether you confirmed the signup, and related subscription records. MailerLite may also record email delivery and engagement events, such as opens, clicks, and unsubscribes.
  • Website use: the site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use its pages. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as pages viewed, browser or device details, and interaction data. Your browser settings can limit cookies.
  • Messages and bookings: if you contact me, I use the details and information you choose to provide to respond. If you book or pay for a session, Calendly and Stripe handle information needed for those services under their own privacy notices.
  • Technical information: when a page loads, your browser sends technical information to the services that deliver the site. Those services may process details such as an IP address, browser information, and the page requested.

How information is used

I use personal information to deliver the guide you request, confirm your signup, send the six-email follow-up series described on the signup form, respond to inquiries, manage bookings or payments you choose to make, understand website use, and protect the website and services.

Email updates and your choices

The free-guide signup uses a confirmation email. After you confirm, MailerLite sends the guide and the follow-up email series. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe option. You can unsubscribe at any time, or contact me using the details below. I may keep a minimal record of an unsubscribe so I can respect your choice.

Service providers and overseas handling

The website relies on service providers to operate these functions. MailerLite manages the signup form, confirmation, subscriber records, and emails. Google Analytics measures website use. Calendly supports bookings, and Stripe processes payments when you choose to book a paid session. Website hosting and security providers may also process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site.

These providers may store or process information outside Australia. Their own privacy notices explain their handling practices. I do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. If you block them, some analytics information may not be collected.

Storage and security

I keep personal information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described above, to manage our relationship, or to meet legal obligations. Service providers may retain limited records under their own retention and backup practices. Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access, correction, and privacy concerns

You can ask to access or correct personal information I hold about you, request its deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy concern by emailing mario@mariotabone.com.au. I will review and respond to your request. If you are not satisfied with my response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when my practices or services change. The latest version will always be published on this page with its update date.
